## Observed failures fixed during the build

* Curated teaching example initially taken from a backtest trade (fold-frozen z = −3.23) disagreed with the playback refit at that date (z = −1.72,
  not eligible). Selection was rewritten to use the same as-of refit logic.
* Reveal reported an "exit-band crossing" when the as-of z was already inside ±0.5. It now reports n/a with a reason.
* Mobile: navigation row and long exclusion tokens widened the layout viewport (35 px and 116–127 px overflow). Fixed with min-width and wrapping rules, verified by Playwright.
* PNG card caveat overflowed and band labels collided with the line. Re-laid out and visually re-checked.
* Negative rupee values rendered as "₹-1,168". Now "−₹1,168".
* Drawdown was computed in the browser. Moved to the backend ledger (backend numbers are authoritative).
* Test-construction errors (half-life truth vs sample estimate, a degenerate-σ fixture, a vacuous holdout assertion) were corrected; the half-life
  test now compares against an independent statsmodels OLS.
